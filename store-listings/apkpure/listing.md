## Full Description

TV Viewer is a free, open-source IPTV streaming application that lets you watch live TV and listen to radio stations from around the world.

🌍 **Community Catalog** — Browse live TV and radio from around the world
📺 **Categories** — News, Sports, Entertainment, Music, Kids, Movies & more
🔍 **Search & Filter** — Find channels by name, country, or category
⭐ **Favorites** — Save your preferred channels for quick access
📻 **Radio Mode** — Dedicated radio player with background playback
🔊 **Background Audio** — Keep listening when you switch apps
🚫 **No Ads** — Completely ad-free experience
🔒 **Privacy** — No account required; optional analytics and community sharing are controlled in Settings

Community sharing sends URL-hashed health reports and fetches shared channel-health feedback. URL hashes may match known streams. Without sharing, channels without a local check show Unknown; the public channel catalog and local checks remain available.

The app connects to publicly available M3U playlists and HLS streams. All streaming content is provided by third-party sources.
