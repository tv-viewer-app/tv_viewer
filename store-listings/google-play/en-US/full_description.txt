Browse a community-maintained catalog of free IPTV streams from around the world — no sign-up, no subscription, no ads.

TV Viewer connects you to a community-powered database of publicly available live TV and radio streams. Browse by category, search by name, and start watching instantly.

🌍 CHANNELS FROM EVERYWHERE
• Live TV and radio streams from around the world
• Live TV, sports, news, movies, entertainment
• Anime channels — HIDIVE, Pluto TV Anime, MAX Anime & more
• Free-to-air TV from Germany, Israel, US, India & beyond
• Radio stations for background listening

📺 POWERFUL PLAYER
• Built-in HLS/HTTP video player
• Multi-source fallback — if one stream fails, try another automatically
• Picture-in-Picture mode for multitasking
• Background audio for radio stations
• Channel health status comes from a local check or, when community sharing is enabled, shared feedback

🔍 FIND WHAT YOU WANT
• Smart search across all channels
• Quick category filters: Sports, News, Movies, Kids, Music, Anime
• Watch Now — curated sections with popular and trending channels
• Favorites — save channels for quick access

🛡️ PRIVACY FIRST
• No account required
• Optional analytics is off by default and may include channel names, URL hashes, and a random install identifier
• Separate optional community sharing sends URL-hashed health reports and fetches shared health feedback
• URL hashes can be matched to known streams; newly contributed channels may include stream URLs
• No ads or advertising SDKs
• Open source (MIT License)

📊 COMMUNITY POWERED
• Enable community sharing to send health reports and receive shared status feedback
• With sharing off, channels without a local check show Unknown; local checks and the public catalog remain available
• New channels added regularly from community sources
• Community statistics dashboard

⚡ RELIABLE STREAMING
• Smart channel sorting — reliable streams first
• Automatic source fallback when streams go down
• Channel status uses local checks or shared reports when community sharing is enabled

Also available as a web app (Docker) and desktop app (Windows/Linux).

Source code: https://github.com/tv-viewer-app/tv_viewer

Note: TV Viewer aggregates publicly available streams from community-maintained repositories. We do not host any content. Channel availability depends on the source providers.
