TV Viewer is a free, open-source IPTV streaming app built around community-maintained playlists and privacy-first design.

Features:
• Live TV and radio channels from community-maintained sources
• Community Statistics page with anonymous aggregated usage insights
• Timezone-based country detection (no GPS or IP lookup needed)
• Background audio playback for radio streams
• Multi-source failover when one stream stops working
• Search, favorites, source selector, and watch history
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support where available
• No account required and no ads
• Optional usage and playback analytics (opt-in only)
• Community sharing sends URL-hashed health reports and fetches shared
  channel-health feedback; when off, no shared health data is exchanged.
  New playlist channels may be shared with their stream URLs when enabled
• Self-hosted web/Docker edition also available
• Regular updates with new channels and fixes from the community

Privacy:
• No account or contact details collected
• No account or login required
• Analytics are off by default; optional playback events include channel names
  and URL hashes that can be matched to known streams
• In Settings, community sharing controls both sending health reports and
  fetching shared health feedback; URL hashes may match known stream URLs
• Country detection uses device timezone only
• All source code is open and auditable

This app does not host or provide any streams. It aggregates publicly available M3U playlists maintained by the open-source community (iptv-org and others). Channel availability depends on the source providers.

Network services:
• Channel playlists are fetched from community and third-party hosts
• FMStream.org is queried for public radio-station listings
• Supabase provides the shared channel database, aggregated community statistics, optional analytics, and URL-hashed channel-health exchange
• Usage analytics is separate and not required for shared health feedback;
  community sharing is required to send reports and fetch that feedback

Permissions:
• Internet: Required to stream content
• Wake Lock: Keeps playback active when needed
• Foreground Service: Background audio playback notification
