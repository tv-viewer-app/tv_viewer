TV Viewer is a free, open-source IPTV streaming app built around community-maintained playlists and privacy-first design.

Features:
• 16,000+ live TV and radio channels from around the world
• Community Statistics page with anonymous aggregated usage insights
• Timezone-based country detection (no GPS or IP lookup needed)
• Background audio playback for radio streams
• Multi-source failover when one stream stops working
• EPG (Electronic Program Guide) with current/next program info
• Search, favorites, source selector, and watch history
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) support where available
• No account required, no ads, no tracking by default
• Optional anonymous usage analytics (opt-in only)
• Self-hosted web/Docker edition also available
• Regular updates with new channels and fixes from the community

Privacy:
• No personal data collected
• No account or login required
• Analytics are opt-in and anonymous
• Country detection uses device timezone only
• All source code is open and auditable

This app does not host or provide any streams. It aggregates publicly available M3U playlists maintained by the open-source community (iptv-org and others). Channel availability depends on the source providers.

Permissions:
• Internet: Required to stream content
• Wake Lock: Keeps playback active when needed
• Foreground Service: Background audio playback notification
